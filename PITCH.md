# Hospital Management SaaS — Buyer Pitch

## The Problem
Small private hospitals in India (20–200 beds) still manage patient
intake, triage, and billing on paper or Excel. Wrong triage classification
sends urgent patients to wait — a patient safety issue. Manual appointment
booking loses patients to competitors with digital booking.

## The Solution
A production-ready SaaS with:
- Real-time AI triage (type "chest pain" → red EMERGENCY badge instantly)
- 10-department auto-routing so the right specialist sees the right patient
- Full EHR: appointments, vitals, diagnoses, prescriptions, billing
- White-label: your hospital's name, logo, and colors in 5 minutes

## Live Example
Doctor types: "chest pain radiating to left arm"
System responds: EMERGENCY → routes to Cardiology → assigns Dr. Rajesh Kumar
Result: Patient gets seen immediately, not after a 2-hour queue

## Market
Bangalore alone: 400+ private hospitals, 2,000+ clinics.
India: 70,000+ private hospitals. Target: 20–200 bed facilities.
